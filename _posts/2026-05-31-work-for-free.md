title: You should work for free*

Hear me out before you get angry

The biggest software companies in the world have a free product or a free trial

Domino's became the biggest pizza company by letting you get a free pizza if they're late

Free is the absolute fastest way to grow if you can master it

But most people are scared

They reject free work because most free work is structured badly

What you should never do is work for free with no defined return

When someone asks you to work for exposure, do not accept “I’ll give you exposure.”

Say: introduce me to five business owners who buy this kind of work, with a personal recommendation, right after delivery.

A free internship is not the end of the world, if there is a great project, a named mentor, and a clear path to turning it into a career, 

Free shouldn't mean they pay nothing. It just means they don't pay money.

You need to trade for something that is helpful to you. Free is just barter for some other helpful things.

Google, Instagram let you use their service for free in exchange of showing you ads

Uber used to give free credits, chatgpt and claude give you intelligence for free.

People give away books and useful information because they want the right to sell to you later.

You get business content for free if you just hit that follow button.

Free is a trade, you should get something in return, it need not always be money.

So don't let people exploit you, but you should work for free.
